The Weight of What You Learned
You didn't know
what you didn't know.
And the page was so, so empty.
Then everything came at once.
Too much. Too fast.
You couldn't hold it all.
No one told you that was normal.
You practiced.
And nothing changed.
day 1
You did the same thing yesterday.
You'll do it again tomorrow.
The progress you were promised
doesn't seem to know your name.
You wondered if you were broken...

if everyone else got a manual
that you somehow missed.
And then—

not with thunder,
not with applause,

but in a quiet moment
when you weren't even trying—
it clicked.
Not everything. Not perfectly.

But enough.
Enough to know
you weren't broken after all.
You couldn't explain
what changed.
But your hands knew.
Your instincts knew.
The knowledge didn't arrive like a download.
It arrived like a season—
you couldn't point to the day
winter became spring.
But you were different now.
Someday someone will sit
where you sat.
Staring at the blank page.
Drowning in the flood.
Practicing with no proof
it's working.
And they will look at you—
terrified, uncertain—
and you will say:

"I know. I know.
Keep going."
